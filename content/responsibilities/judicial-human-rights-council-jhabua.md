## District President — Judicial Human Rights Council, Jhabua

Advocate Vagesh Samvedi serves as the District President of the Judicial Human Rights Council, Jhabua, Madhya Pradesh.

His association with the organisation reflects an engagement with the broader principles of human dignity, equality, justice and awareness of individual rights.

Human rights form an essential foundation of a fair and inclusive society. Awareness of constitutional values, legal protections and individual freedoms plays an important role in strengthening public understanding of rights and responsibilities.

Through this organisational responsibility, his involvement is directed towards encouraging awareness of human rights, supporting community-oriented initiatives and promoting a greater understanding of the importance of justice and equality.

His professional background in law provides an additional perspective on the relationship between legal awareness, individual rights and social responsibility.

The role represents an important dimension of his engagement beyond independent legal practice.
