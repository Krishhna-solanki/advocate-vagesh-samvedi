## Joint Secretary — Boxing Association, Jhabua

Advocate Vagesh Samvedi serves as the Joint Secretary of the Boxing Association, Jhabua, contributing to the organisational and administrative dimension of sports-related activities.

Sports play an important role in developing discipline, determination, physical fitness, teamwork and confidence among young people. Boxing, in particular, requires dedication, self-control, perseverance and respect for sporting discipline.

His association with the Boxing Association reflects an interest in sports administration, youth participation and the development of sporting opportunities within the community.

Through this organisational responsibility, he is associated with the broader objective of encouraging sports participation and supporting initiatives that recognise the importance of physical development and sporting discipline.

This role represents another dimension of his engagement with society, extending beyond his legal profession into organisational responsibilities and community participation.
