A small trader who had been struggling for nearly three years to access ₹4,56,341 blocked in his bank account due to a Cyber Lien imposed by the Cyber Department has received significant legal relief.

## Three years of a blocked account

Despite making repeated efforts through the bank, concerned authorities and various administrative channels, the trader was unable to get the blocked amount released. The prolonged restriction adversely affected his business operations and the financial well-being of his family.

## Writ petition before the High Court

Eventually, the trader approached Advocate Vagesh Samvedi for legal assistance. After examining the matter in detail, Advocate Samvedi filed a writ petition before the Hon'ble Madhya Pradesh High Court under Article 21 of the Constitution of India.

The petition highlighted that keeping a person's legally earned money blocked for several years could adversely affect their constitutional right to life and livelihood.

## Order for removal of the Cyber Lien

Following the hearing before the Hon'ble High Court and the subsequent judicial proceedings, an order concerning the removal of the Cyber Lien was passed by the Senior Judicial Magistrate.

Following the court's order, the bank will now proceed with the necessary formalities for releasing the blocked amount.

## The trader's response

Expressing happiness over the development, the trader stated that despite making continuous efforts for nearly three years, he had not received any relief. He credited Advocate Vagesh Samvedi's active intervention, effective legal representation and timely legal action for bringing hope of justice.

The trader also expressed gratitude to both Hon'ble Courts and hoped that the order would be implemented promptly, enabling him to recover his hard-earned money.

## Why it matters

This case highlights the importance of appropriate legal remedies and effective representation in matters involving prolonged Cyber Lien restrictions. It demonstrates how legal proceedings can help individuals seek relief even in cases that have remained unresolved for years.
