On the occasion of National Sports Day, Kabaddi and Kho-Kho competitions were organized at the sports ground of Sandipani School in Petlawad, Madhya Pradesh. Students and young athletes participated enthusiastically, demonstrating their sporting abilities, teamwork and competitive spirit.

## Guests

The event was attended by several distinguished guests, including Ashish Baviskar, State Vice President of the Human Rights Council; Advocate Vagesh Samvedi, District President of the Human Rights Council; Ankit Bhandari, Secretary of the Jhabua Boxing Association; and Puralal Chauhan, Principal of Sandipani School.

The guests interacted with the participating athletes and encouraged them to pursue sports with dedication and discipline. They highlighted the importance of sports alongside education, emphasizing their role in developing confidence, leadership, discipline and team spirit among young people.

## Results

In the boys' Kabaddi category, Yuva Kabaddi Club Petlawad secured first place, while Model School Petlawad finished as runners-up.

In the girls' Kho-Kho category, Sandipani School Petlawad's team secured the winning position, while another team from the same school finished as runners-up.

## Awards and recognition

During the closing ceremony, the winning and runner-up teams were presented with shields and pens by the guests.

Athletes who had participated in national and state-level sporting competitions were also recognized. These included netball players Shubham Amliyar and Udaysingh Amliyar, along with Kho-Kho players Asha Khadiya and Papita Khadiya.

## Conduct of the programme

The programme was conducted by Hemraj Ganawa, Block Youth Coordinator, Department of Sports and Youth Welfare, Petlawad. He encouraged young people to participate regularly in sporting activities and continue developing their abilities.

The event concluded with a vote of thanks by sports teacher Y. D. Purohit, who expressed gratitude to the guests, athletes and everyone involved in organizing the programme.
