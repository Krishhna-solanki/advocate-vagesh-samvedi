An 82-year-old retired bank manager who lost ₹41,805 to cyber fraudsters has had the amount returned. *Dainik Crime Darpan* reports that the relief came through the promptness of Petlawad Police, the legal initiative of Advocate Vagesh Samvedi and an order of the court.

## The fraud

According to the report, unknown cyber fraudsters contacted the victim through a phone call, obtained the victim's banking details and fraudulently withdrew ₹41,805 from the victim's bank account.

## Complaint lodged under legal guidance

On learning of the fraud, the victim contacted Advocate Vagesh Samvedi. Under the advocate's guidance, a complaint was lodged at Petlawad police station.

## Prompt action by Petlawad Police

Petlawad Police acted promptly and had the related transaction put on hold, which stopped the amount from being transferred any further.

## Court order returns the amount

Advocate Vagesh Samvedi then took the necessary legal proceedings before the court. Following these proceedings, the Judicial Magistrate First Class, Petlawad, directed that the amount on hold be returned to the victim's account.

> "This case proves that with a timely complaint, active policing and the strength of the law, the schemes of cyber fraudsters can be defeated."
>
> — Advocate Vagesh Samvedi, as quoted in *Dainik Crime Darpan* (translated from Hindi)

## If you are a victim of cyber fraud

The report carries a message for the public: do not panic if you fall victim to cyber fraud — act immediately.

- **Call 1930**, the National Cyber Helpline.
- **Register a complaint online** on the Cyber Crime Portal, [www.cybercrime.gov.in](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in).
- **File a complaint at your nearest police station** to ensure immediate action.

*Stay alert, stay safe — your awareness is your security.*
