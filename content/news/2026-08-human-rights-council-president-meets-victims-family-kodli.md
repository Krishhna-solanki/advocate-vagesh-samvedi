Bhanwar Singh Shekhawat, State President of the Judicial Human Rights Council, visited village Kodli and met a family affected by an incident, *Anchal Express* reported on 7 August 2026. The council's delegation also met the Collector and demanded that the matter be heard in a fast-track court so that the family receives speedy justice.

## Visit to the family in Kodli

According to the report, datelined Jhabua, Shekhawat reached Kodli and met the affected family. A photograph published with the report shows the State President and members of the council in discussion with the family and consoling them.

## Meeting with the Collector

The council's delegation then met the Collector. A photograph caption in the report shows office-bearers of the council handing over a memorandum during the meeting.

## Demand for a fast-track hearing

The delegation demanded a hearing in a fast-track court and speedy justice for the affected family.

## Members of the delegation

The newspaper lists the following among those present in the delegation, together with other office-bearers and members of the council:

- Bhanwar Singh Shekhawat, State President, Judicial Human Rights Council
- Ashish Baviskar, State Vice-President
- Advocate Vagesh Samvedi
- Tulsiram Rathore
- Sharad Dave
