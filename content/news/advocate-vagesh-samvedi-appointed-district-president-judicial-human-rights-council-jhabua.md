The Judicial Human Rights Council, Madhya Pradesh has appointed Advocate Vagesh Samvedi as District President of the council for Jhabua district, Madhya Pradesh.

## The appointment

According to the newspaper report, the appointment was made as part of expanding the organisation and making the council's work on justice, human rights and social concerns more effective at the district level.

## The nomination letter

The council's nomination letter (मनोनयन पत्र), reproduced in the clipping, nominates Advocate Vagesh Samvedi as District President, Jhabua (Madhya Pradesh). As stated in the letter and the report, the appointment was made as directed by the council's Madhya Pradesh State President, Bhanwar Singh Shekhawat, and by State Working President Ashish Baviskar as part of appointing the organisation's office-bearers. The letter is signed by Ashish Baviskar.

## The council's expectations

In entrusting Advocate Samvedi with the role, the council expressed the expectation that under his leadership the Jhabua district executive would be made stronger, more active and better organised, and that effective work would be done to take issues of justice, human rights and social equality to every citizen.

## Advocate Samvedi's response

Thanking the council's senior office-bearers for the appointment, Advocate Samvedi said he would carry out the responsibility entrusted to him with priority to impartiality, constitutional values, legal awareness and the public interest. He said that active efforts would be made at the district level to make ordinary citizens aware of their rights and to provide legal and social assistance to people in need.

The council conveyed its best wishes to Advocate Samvedi for the new responsibility, and the report notes the expectation that the appointment will bring new momentum to the council's organisational work in Jhabua district.
