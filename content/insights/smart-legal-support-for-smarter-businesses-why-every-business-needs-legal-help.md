At Law Astra and Associates, we provide dedicated legal services for businesses of all sizes, helping you avoid pitfalls, resolve problems quickly, and grow with confidence.

Why Do Businesses Need Legal Support?  
Whether you’re just starting or scaling up, legal support ensures you’re protected at every step. Here’s how:

1\. Business Formation & Compliance  
Choosing the right structure — sole proprietorship, partnership, LLP, or private limited company — has long-term legal and tax implications. We help you register your business the right way and stay compliant with all legal and government regulations.

2\. Drafting and Reviewing Contracts  
From vendor agreements to employment contracts, we ensure your documents are legally sound and protect your business interests. Poorly drafted contracts can lead to costly disputes later — don’t take that chance.

3\. Dispute Resolution and Arbitration  
Disputes are part of doing business, but going to court is not always the best solution. Our arbitration services help you settle matters out of court, saving time and money. Whether it’s a contract issue, payment delay, or partnership dispute, we’re here to resolve it swiftly and fairly.

4\. Employee and HR Legal Matters  
Hiring? Firing? Handling a complaint? We help you create solid HR policies and handle employment law matters with sensitivity and professionalism. Avoid lawsuits by getting legal clarity from day one.

5\. Data Privacy and Intellectual Property  
Your brand name, logo, website content, software — all need protection. We guide you in registering trademarks, securing copyrights, and ensuring data privacy laws are followed.
