## Overview

The article argues that businesses of any size face legal risks — in contracts, compliance and employee matters — and that ongoing legal support is worth having rather than seeking help only once a problem has arisen.

## Areas covered

- **Business formation and compliance** — choosing a legal structure, registration and meeting regulatory requirements.
- **Contract drafting and review** — vendor, employment and other agreements that protect the business.
- **Dispute resolution** — arbitration as an alternative to court for contract, payment and partnership disagreements.
- **HR and employment matters** — policy creation and guidance on hiring, termination and complaints.
- **Intellectual property and data privacy** — trademark and copyright registration and privacy-law compliance.

## Working remotely

The article also notes that much of this support can be delivered online — contract reviews, virtual arbitration, remote document drafting and guidance by video call, messaging or email.
