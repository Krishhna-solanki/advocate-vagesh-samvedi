What is Arbitration?  
Arbitration is a form of alternative dispute resolution (ADR) where a neutral third party — known as an arbitrator — listens to both sides and makes a decision, just like a judge would. But unlike court proceedings, arbitration is private, less formal, and much faster.

It is legally binding and recognised by law, making it a reliable and respected method for resolving conflicts.

Why Choose Arbitration?  
Here are some of the key benefits of arbitration over traditional litigation:

1\. Time-Efficient  
Court cases can take months or even years to reach a conclusion. Arbitration, on the other hand, is structured to deliver results in a shorter time frame — often in a matter of weeks or a few months.

2\. Cost-Effective  
Legal proceedings can be expensive, especially with extended court hearings and procedural delays. Arbitration reduces these costs significantly by cutting down on time, paperwork, and unnecessary court visits.

3\. Confidentiality  
Unlike court cases that become part of the public record, arbitration proceedings are completely private, protecting your personal or business reputation.
