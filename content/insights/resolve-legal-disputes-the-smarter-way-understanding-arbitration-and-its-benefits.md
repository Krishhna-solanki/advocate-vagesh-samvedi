## Overview

This article introduces arbitration as an alternative to resolving disputes through the courts. It contrasts lengthy and costly court proceedings with arbitration, which it describes as a form of alternative dispute resolution (ADR) in which a neutral arbitrator hears both sides and decides the matter.

## Benefits discussed

The article sets out five considerations that lead parties to choose arbitration:

- **Speed** — proceedings can conclude faster than conventional litigation.
- **Cost** — the process can be less expensive overall.
- **Confidentiality** — arbitration takes place privately rather than in open court.
- **Flexibility** — sessions can be arranged to suit the parties, including online.
- **Experienced counsel** — parties can be assisted by advocates familiar with the process.
