## Why law

The article begins with who the profession suits: people who enjoy debate, untangling complex issues and the pursuit of justice.

## The B.A. LL.B.

It describes the five-year integrated B.A. LL.B. programme and the subjects it covers, including constitutional, criminal, contract and corporate law.

## Entrance examinations

CLAT is identified as the most common entrance examination, with AILET and state-level tests as alternatives. These examinations test English, general knowledge, mathematics and legal aptitude.

## Choosing a law school

National Law Universities (NLUs) are highlighted, alongside other reputed institutions.

## Practical experience

Moot courts, internships and legal aid clinics are presented as essential parts of a legal education.

## After graduation

To practise, graduates must clear the Bar Council of India examination. Beyond that, the article mentions specialisation, higher studies and opportunities abroad — closing on the note that the effort is worth it.
